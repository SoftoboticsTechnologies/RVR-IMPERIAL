About sourcing from India with RVR Imperial EXIM

We offer a multi-category sourcing and trading portfolio across seven categories: Agriculture, Food & Agro Commodities; Textiles, Apparel & Fashion; FMCG, Personal Care & Home Care; Beauty, Herbal & Wellness Products; Home, Lifestyle & Handicrafts; Packaging, Plastics & Polymer Products; and Machinery, Tools & Industrial Supplies. Product availability, specifications, minimum order quantities, and supply arrangements are confirmed according to individual requirements and applicable regulations.

We serve importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, corporate buyers, brand owners, and institutional buyers across diverse industries, and welcome opportunities to develop mutually beneficial trade relationships.
