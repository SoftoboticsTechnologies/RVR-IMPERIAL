How We Work

From Inquiry to Delivery in 6 Coordinated Steps

From identifying suitable products to coordinating shipment requirements, we work to make cross-border trade more straightforward, efficient, and commercially valuable.

  1. 01

    Share Your Requirements

    Share your product requirements, target market, estimated order quantity, and specifications.

  2. 02

    Product Sourcing & Supplier Matching

    We identify suitable products and potential supply partners.

  3. 03

    Tailored Quotation (Incoterms)

    Prices are quoted individually by category, grade, specifications, quantity, packaging, availability, and agreed Incoterms.

  4. 04

    Specifications, Packaging & Private Label

    We coordinate buyer-specific packaging and labelling requirements.

  5. 05

    Order & Quality Coordination

    We coordinate orders according to agreed quantities and specifications.

  6. 06

    Export Documentation & Shipment

    We coordinate documentation and shipment processes with relevant service providers.
