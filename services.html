Our Services

Comprehensive Trade Solutions. One Reliable Partner.

At RVR Imperial EXIM Private Limited, we go beyond conventional import-export trading to deliver integrated global sourcing, product supply, and customized trade solutions.

Rooted in India and focused on international markets, we connect businesses with sourcing opportunities across diverse product categories. We work with buyers, importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and brand owners to facilitate efficient procurement, flexible supply arrangements, and long-term business partnerships.

From agricultural commodities and food products to apparel, consumer goods, lifestyle products, and industrial supplies, our diversified portfolio is designed to address evolving business requirements across global markets.

Whether you are sourcing products for distribution, expanding your product portfolio, or developing your own brand, we aim to make international trade more accessible, coordinated, and commercially rewarding.

Commercial Terms

Common Incoterms & Shipment Options

Let us know your preferred Incoterm when you enquire, or choose “Not sure” and we will discuss a suitable option. Definitions below are a simplified guide based on Incoterms® 2020.

EXW
Ex WorksGoods are made available at the agreed place; the buyer arranges onward transport.
FOB
Free On BoardGoods are delivered on board the vessel at the named port of shipment.
CFR
Cost and FreightSeller pays freight to the named destination port; risk transfers on loading.
CIF
Cost, Insurance and FreightAs CFR, with marine insurance arranged to the destination port.
DAP
Delivered At PlaceGoods are delivered to the named destination, ready for unloading.
FCL
LCL
FCL & LCL ShipmentsShipment type is planned around your order volume — a full container (FCL) for larger orders, or shared container space (LCL) where suitable, coordinated with logistics partners.
